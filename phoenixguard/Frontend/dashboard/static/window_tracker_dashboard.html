How to read this page

Three things stay separate

01
Current movement

What price is doing in the newest valid chart observation. An earlier move is shown as history, not as current pressure.

02
Likely next

A forecast is a watched direction. It can differ from the current movement and never grants a trade by itself.

03
Entry status

WAIT remains the default. BUY NOW means look for a lower price inside the verified demand or retest area; SELL NOW means look for a higher price inside the verified supply or retest area. Never chase a move.

04
Interactive overlays

Use a quick view or switch any layer in the Overlay Library. SMC, Two-candle, LSTM, structure, zones, plans, and history remain independent. Select a mark for its plain-language meaning.

A setup window can remain available for several minutes while Phoenix Guard refreshes current-frame permission. It closes early whenever live truth invalidates the setup. Visual analysis is decision support, not a promise of market outcome.
