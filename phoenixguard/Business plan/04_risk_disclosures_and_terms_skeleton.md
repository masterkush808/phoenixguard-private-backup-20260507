Trading foreign exchange, contracts for difference, commodities, indices, crypto assets, binary options, and other leveraged products involves substantial risk. You can lose some or all of your deposited funds. You should not trade money that you cannot afford to lose.

Past performance, backtests, demonstrations, burn tests, screenshots, testimonials, model scores, signal accuracy, or historical examples do not guarantee future results.

PhoenixGuard may produce incorrect, delayed, stale, incomplete, or unsuitable signals. Market conditions can change faster than software can react. Broker execution, spreads, slippage, requotes, platform outages, latency, liquidity, and market closures can materially affect results.

By using PhoenixGuard, you accept full responsibility for all trading decisions, all EA settings, all risk controls, all broker activity, and all resulting profits or losses.

## No Financial Advice

PhoenixGuard does not provide personalized financial, investment, tax, legal, or accounting advice. Any analysis, signal, command, model output, or automation behavior is provided for informational and software-assistance purposes only.

You are responsible for deciding whether PhoenixGuard is suitable for your circumstances, risk tolerance, broker, jurisdiction, and account size.

## No Guarantee

PhoenixGuard does not guarantee:

- profitable trades.
- winning percentage.
- account protection.
- no drawdown.
- no account blowup.
- uninterrupted service.
- execution at displayed price.
- compatibility with every broker.
- compliance with every country's laws.

## Customer Responsibilities

The customer must:

- understand market risk.
- configure EA settings responsibly.
- use conservative lot sizing.
- monitor the terminal and broker account.
- keep MT4 and broker access secure.
- confirm broker permissions for automated trading.
- comply with local laws and broker terms.
- stop using PhoenixGuard if they do not understand the risks.

## Broker And Execution Risk

PhoenixGuard does not control the broker. The broker may reject, delay, reprice, slip, close, or fail orders. The broker may widen spreads, disable trading, change symbols, use suffixes, or apply margin rules that affect execution.

## Software And Availability Risk

PhoenixGuard may fail due to:

- internet outage.
- cloud outage.
- customer PC outage.
- MT4 error.
- Windows permission issue.
- connector failure.
- API latency.
- stale data.
- malformed packet.
- market closure.
- broker trade restrictions.

The system must fail closed where possible, but no software can eliminate all failure modes.
